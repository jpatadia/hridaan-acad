# NJSLA Math Coach 🧮

An adaptive daily math coach for Hridaan, built to help him **excel on the NJSLA Grade 5 math test** and keep pushing into **Grade 6** content.

- **10 questions every day**, chosen fresh based on how he has been doing.
- **Adaptive:** each skill has 5 levels, and the level moves up or down with his answers.
- **Wrong answers turn into lessons:** first a hint and a second try, then a full **step-by-step guide**.
- **Parent dashboard:** level and accuracy for every skill, a daily score chart, and every missed question with its solution.
- No account, no server, no install, no ads. It is a single web page that runs in any browser, including on a tablet.

## Using it

**Quickest way:** open `index.html` in a browser (double-click it).

**On a tablet or any device:** host it with GitHub Pages (free):
1. Merge this branch into `main`.
2. In the repo, go to **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: GitHub Actions**.
3. The included workflow runs the tests and publishes the site at `https://<your-username>.github.io/hridaan-acad/`. Bookmark it or add it to the home screen.

Progress is saved in the browser on that device. Use **Settings → Export progress** for a backup, and **Import** to move to another device.

## What the 10 daily questions are

| Slot | Count | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Warm-up | 2 | Spaced review of skills he is strong in, so they stay sharp |
| 🟠 Fix it | 3 | Skills he missed in the last 7 days, or his weakest skills |
| 🔵 Level up | 3 | Skills still climbing, at his current level |
| ⭐ Challenge | 2 | **One level above** his current level. Getting it right jumps him straight up |

Day 1 acts as a diagnostic across all Grade 5 skills, starting at the "NJSLA ready" level. After finishing, he can ask for **5 bonus challenge questions**.

## How it adapts

Each skill tracks a **level (1–5)** and a **rating** (recent accuracy).

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Warm-up (Grade 5 basics) |
| 2 | On grade |
| 3 | **NJSLA ready**: multi-step and tricky, test-style |
| 4 | Advanced |
| 5 | **Grade 6 challenge** (e.g. fraction ÷ fraction, decimal ÷ decimal, negative coordinates, evaluating expressions with variables) |

- 2 first-try correct in a row → **level up**
- 2 misses in a row → **level down**, rebuilding at an easier level and then climbing back
- A correct ⭐ Challenge → **jump** to that level. A missed challenge never lowers his level.
- Right on the 2nd try (after the hint) earns half credit and doesn't change the level.
- Questions are generated at the moment they are shown, so a level-up earlier in the session makes later questions harder that same day.

**Grade 6 skills unlock automatically** once their Grade 5 prerequisites reach level 4 ("Advanced"):

| Grade 6 skill | Unlocks after |
|---|---|
| Ratios, Rates & Percents (6.RP) | Multiplying Fractions + Division |
| Negative Numbers & Absolute Value (6.NS.5-7) | Coordinate Plane |
| One-Step & Two-Step Equations (6.EE.5-7) | Order of Operations & Expressions |
| Area of Triangles, Parallelograms & Trapezoids (6.G.1) | Volume + Multiplying Fractions |
| Mean, Median & Range (6.SP) | Decimals + Division |
| GCF, LCM & Prime Factorization (6.NS.4) | Adding Fractions + Multiplication |

## Grade 5 skills covered (NJSLA / NJSLS Math)

| Domain | Skills |
|---|---|
| Number & Operations in Base Ten | Place value & powers of 10 (5.NBT.1-4), multi-digit multiplication (5.NBT.5), division (5.NBT.6), decimal operations (5.NBT.7) |
| Number & Operations – Fractions | Add/subtract with unlike denominators & mixed numbers (5.NF.1-2), multiply fractions/area/scaling (5.NF.4-6), divide with unit fractions (5.NF.7) |
| Operations & Algebraic Thinking | Order of operations with ( ) and [ ], writing/interpreting expressions (5.OA.1-2), numerical patterns (5.OA.3) |
| Measurement & Data | Volume of prisms & composite figures (5.MD.3-5), unit conversions (5.MD.1) |
| Geometry | Coordinate plane (5.G.1-2), classifying 2-D figures (5.G.3-4) |

Fractions and Base Ten carry the most weight on the NJSLA, so they come up more often. Questions are **generated by code, not picked from a fixed list**, so he never runs out and can't memorize answers. Every question comes with its own worked solution. Answers can be typed as whole numbers (`1,200`), decimals (`3.75`), fractions (`3/4`) or mixed numbers (`2 1/3`). Fraction answers must be in simplest form; an equal but unsimplified answer gets a friendly "simplify it" nudge with no penalty.

## Project layout

```
index.html        page shell
styles.css        styles (light + dark mode, phone-friendly)
js/util.js        seeded random numbers, fractions, answer parsing/checking
js/skills.js      the 19 skills: question generators, hints, step-by-step solutions
js/engine.js      adaptive engine: levels, unlocks, daily plan, scoring
js/app.js         user interface
tests/run.js      tests (node tests/run.js)
```

The tests generate 400 questions for every skill at every level and check that each one is well formed, that its answer is accepted when typed, and that the answer appears in the steps. They also simulate strong, struggling and fraction-weak students over several weeks to confirm the engine adapts.

### Adding a new skill
Add an entry to `js/skills.js` with an `id`, `name`, `standard`, `domain`, `grade`, `weight` (how often it appears) and a `gen(level, rng)` function that returns `{ prompt, answer, hint, steps }`. For Grade 6 skills, also add `prereqs`. Run `npm test`.
